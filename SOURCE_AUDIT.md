**Copy rule.** Narrative text is selected directly from the PDF, with original sentence wording retained. Extraction-related spaces, line wrapping and hyphenation may be normalized. Figures and their scope are retained: research expenditure is not combined with school revenue, giving is not added to revenue, and the investment amount is not represented as an annual total.

**Photo rule.** Photographs, renderings and report graphics are extracted from the supplied PDF; they are not substituted with stock photographs. The PDF's own image size limits the highest available resolution of some portraits and illustrations. The 59 images used by the final site are re-extracted at native PDF dimensions and encoded as optimized WebP files (quality 84). The graduate hero uses its full 2555 × 973 pixels. No upscaling is applied; see `docs/image-audit.json`.

**Interface copy.** Short functional controls, numbered UI steps and accessibility text are written for navigation and do not represent new report claims.

**Review status.** This is the Folio client preview. Source-reference and asset checks accompany functional browser verification; a complete editorial and accessibility sign-off is outside those automated checks.
